## 📖 About the Project

**PustiBondhu AI** is an AI-powered mobile health and nutrition assistant designed to help people make better food decisions based on their health conditions, medical background, medications, and everyday dietary habits.

Most nutrition applications provide generic dietary recommendations without considering individual health conditions or local food cultures. For people in Bangladesh, this creates an additional challenge because many existing platforms do not adequately understand local dishes, ingredients, and eating habits.

PustiBondhu aims to solve this problem by combining AI, nutrition science, and Bangladeshi food knowledge to deliver personalized and understandable food guidance.

## 🎯 Problems We Want to Solve

- **Generic Nutrition Advice:** Existing nutrition applications often ignore individual medical conditions and dietary restrictions.
- **Lack of Local Food Data:** Limited nutrition information about everyday Bangladeshi foods and dishes.
- **Medical Information Gap:** People struggle to understand how their medical conditions and medications relate to their food choices.
- **Complex Health Reports:** Understanding laboratory reports and their nutritional implications can be difficult.
- **Uninformed Food Decisions:** People often lack accessible guidance about what to eat, limit, or avoid.

## 🧠 AI & Machine Learning

The AI service powers the intelligent features of PustiBondhu, including:

- Retrieving relevant medical and nutritional knowledge using RAG.
- Generating personalized food recommendations through LLMs.
- Understanding Bangla and English queries.
- Recognizing food items from images.
- Extracting important information from medical reports.
- Identifying potential food–medicine interactions using reliable references.

## 📦 Project Repositories

PustiBondhu is developed across three separate repositories:

- **AI/ML Engine:** LLM, RAG, food recognition, and report intelligence.
- **Core Backend:** Authentication, user management, nutrition services, and application APIs.
- **Mobile Application:** Flutter-based application for Android and iOS.

## 🛡️ Responsible AI

PustiBondhu AI aims to provide reliable, evidence-based nutritional information.

- Recommendations should be grounded in trustworthy sources.
- AI-generated responses should undergo appropriate validation.
- User medical information should be handled securely.
- Users should remain in control of their food and health decisions.

**Disclaimer:** PustiBondhu AI is intended for informational and educational nutrition guidance only. It does not replace professional medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment.
